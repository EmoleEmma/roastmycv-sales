How it works

  1. Upload your CV. Drop in a PDF, up to 5MB.
  2. Get the roast. An AI model (LLaMA 3.3 70B) scores clarity, ATS, skills, impact and overall, then lists what is weak.
  3. Get the rewrite. One click produces a rewritten CV aimed at ATS systems and tech hiring in Nigeria and abroad.

The whole thing takes under a minute.

What the review gives you

  • Five scores so you can see which part of your CV is dragging you down.
  • Blunt, specific notes on the exact lines that cost you interviews, not generic advice.
  • A full rewrite you can read, edit and send.
  • A tech focus. Feedback is written for software and technology roles, with the Nigerian market in mind.
